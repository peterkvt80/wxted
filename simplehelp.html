Guide to Keyboard commands

These are the special keys that you can use for editing teletext pages. In many cases you can also use edit.tf escapes.

Special keys

KeyAction
Page UpShow next page of carousel
Page DownShow previous page of carousel
F11Hide/Reveal
ctrl-zUndo
esc qToggle show control codes (or Alt-Gr)
